community funding

where support goes.

The first drop is a test of whether collectors want to help develop paradiseön. Here’s what is still open.

How will NFT proceeds be used?

The team is costing the artwork, the mint and the next release of the platform. Before sales open, the budget will show what goes to each and which contributions have been agreed with project partners.

What can a holder take part in?

The proposed Celo NFT represents a Sidi artwork. Community dinners and a vote on a defined project budget are ideas under discussion. Confirmed member benefits will appear with the edition terms.

Is there a community raffle?

One idea is a place on a January journey. The hosts would first need to agree to the visit. Flights would be separate. Dates and entry terms are still open, and the draw has not started.

Siriani Txana Huni Kuin

Adviser to paradiseön

Siriani is a Huni Kuin singer and spiritual leader from Aldeia Segredo do Artesão. Her work with Aîbaibu Kayawai grew from her grandmother Bimi Shu Ikaya’s vision for a women’s association in the village.
